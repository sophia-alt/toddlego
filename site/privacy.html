Privacy policy

Effective October 13, 2026

ToddleGo is an app that helps parents and caregivers find activities for young children nearby. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. In short: there are no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and we do not keep your location.

Information the app uses

  • Your location, if you allow it. With your permission, the app uses your device's location to show activities near you and estimate drive times. To fetch nearby listings, the app sends our database an approximate area around you (a range of map grid cells covering about 10 miles), not your exact position. We do not store your location or connect it to you. You can deny location access and pick a city instead.
  • Your settings. The filters you choose (time frame, age range, indoor only) and the city you pick are saved on your device only.

Information we do not collect

  • No account, name, email address or phone number is needed to use the app.
  • The app has no advertising and no analytics or tracking.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.

Service providers

Activity listings are stored in and served from Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). When the app requests listings, Google processes that request, including your device's IP address, under the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy terms. On Android, your device's location services determine your location.

Links to other sites

Listings link to the organizer's own event pages and to Google Maps. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

ToddleGo is meant for parents and caregivers, not for use by children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above. If the app starts collecting new kinds of information, we will say so here before it does.
