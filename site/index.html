What it does

Near you, right now

Activities within 10 miles of you or a city you pick, nearest first, with an estimated drive time.

Made for ages 0–5

Filter by 0–2 or 2–4 years and indoor only. Only programs for babies, toddlers, preschoolers and families are listed.

Weekly routines

Recurring storytimes are grouped into a Monday-to-Sunday timetable, so it is easy to plan a week.

Always the official page

Every listing links to the organizer's own event page for full details and registration.

For libraries and event organizers

ToddleGo is an independent project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the libraries and organizers whose public events it lists. If you run one of those calendars, here is how we treat your listings:

  • We show the essentials only: title, date and time, location, age group and a short excerpt. We do not copy full descriptions or images.
  • We send families to you: every listing links back to your event page, where they read the details and register.
  • We read published feeds politely: we use the event feeds and calendars you publish, identify ourselves as ToddleGoBot/0.1, follow robots.txt and crawl delays, and fetch at most a few times a day.
  • We fix and remove on request: errors are corrected within 48 hours, and we stop listing your events whenever you ask.

We would love to work with you: to get your permission, use a feed you prefer, or correct anything we show. Please write to plainstudio.dev@gmail.com.
